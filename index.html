01 · The Problem

You are likely running into one of these problems

We get you out of uncomfortable manufacturing situations — with local expertise, local language, and better connections to find the right manufacturer for your product. Select the one you recognise.

Case 01

Looking to manufacture or design a new product?

  • The tooling and suppliers you choose at this moment lock in your cost and quality for years.
  • Design work bought blind from overseas can come back buggy, over-priced and impossible to mass-produce.
  • Founders almost never have China capability of their own, and rarely consider it.
“Initial supplier lock-in cost us 35% in margin over two production cycles.” Hardware founder

Case 02

Can’t scale past your supplier?

  • The factory you are currently using often cannot carry you to real volume.
  • Suppliers overstate capacity and quality — some are traders posing as factories.
  • Switching at volume without a vetted backup puts your continuity at risk, and your IP, designs and tooling sit in your supplier’s hands.
“We chose the wrong supplier at the start, and now we cannot expand properly.” Hardware founder

Case 03

Stuck with the wrong supplier?

  • Are you overpaying, chasing quality, or stuck with an inadequate contract — or a trader posing as a factory?
  • You suspect you are being scammed, but cannot prove it from Europe.
  • Chasing quality from a distance is unreliable, takes weeks, requires flights and local connections.
